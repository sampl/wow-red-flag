# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve [wowredflag.com]. This is a maintainer-led project, so please submit ideas through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sampl/wow-red-flag/issues) rather than opening pull requests.

## What to suggest

We welcome corrections, broken-link reports, accessibility feedback, and proposals for new entries. For substantive changes, explain the problem, proposed wording, and supporting sources.

Suggested content must:

- Describe recurring patterns of behavior, not diagnose people or label individuals.
- Be empathetic, specific, and free of victim-blaming language.
- Use credible sources for factual, clinical, or mental-health claims.
- Avoid sharing identifying personal information about other people.

The maintainer may edit, decline, or defer suggestions to keep the resource accurate, safe, and within scope. Participation is also subject to the [Code of Conduct](CONDUCT.md).
