Wow, red flag...

Do you know someone in a difficult relationship with a friend, family member, partner or co-worker? Are you dealing with a difficult relationship yourself?

Stress is normal in relationships, especially our most intimate ones. Dealing with natural stress in a healthy way is a sign of emotionally maturity.

But sometimes, stress is a sign of something more serious: a person or relationship who repeatedly causes significant pain, and who's behavior is not altered by simply offering care and communication.

This site is a list of behaviors and personalities attributes commonly associated with those people and relationships, so that we all can make better choices about the social fabric we create.
